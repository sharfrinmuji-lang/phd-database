RESEARCH POLICY

Intellectual Property Rights

Administered by the Research & Development Cell, PSGRKCW

Policy Framework

The PSGRKCW Intellectual Property Policy (est. 2017) provides the essential legal framework for protecting innovations generated by our PhD scholars. It balances institutional support with inventor rewards, ensuring your research translates into protected intellectual assets.

Scope and Applicability

Applicable to all PhD students, supervisors, staff, and faculty of PSGRKCW. Covers inventions, teaching materials, software, proprietary algorithms, and all collaborative research projects funded by the institution or external agencies.

Ownership of Intellectual Property

Inventions, patents, and hardware creations made utilizing significant institutional resources are strictly owned by PSGR Krishnammal College for Women. Authors retain full copyright ownership for authored books and academic journal publications.

Revenue Sharing Formula

To foster innovation, net earnings from commercialization and technology licensing are distributed favorably: 70% to the Inventor(s) and 30% to PSGRKCW.

Patent Search and Protection

100% funding support for patent drafting, filing, and legal prosecution costs for innovation proposals approved by the internal IPR Review and Ethics Committee.

Dispute Resolution & Infringement

Any conflicts regarding IP ownership or authorship credit are resolved exclusively by the PSGRKCW IP Grievance Cell. Strict action is taken against plagiarism under the academic integrity code.

Industry Collaboration

For research sponsored by external industries (MoUs), IP rights are handled on a joint-ownership basis or as negotiated in advance, ensuring researchers' contributions are appropriately credited.

Researcher's Note (System Integration)

To ensure your research is eligible for IPR support, PhD scholars must maintain accurate Publication Records and Synopsis Files within this database. Disclosure of any potential invention must occur prior to public presentation or thesis submission to secure patentability.

Download the latest Institutional Handbook for detailed clauses on licensing and "march-in" rights.

Research Incentives Policy

Official PSGRKCW Guidelines for Academic Rewards (Est. 2018)

Policy Framework

The PSGRKCW Research Incentives Policy is designed to encourage and reward the exceptional research efforts of our PhD scholars and faculty. By providing financial support and formal recognition, the institution aims to foster a vibrant, globally competitive academic environment.

Journal Publications

Cash incentives up to ₹10,000 for publishing research papers in high-impact factor, peer-reviewed international journals (Scopus/Web of Science indexed).

Travel Grants

100% financial assistance for Registration, Travel, and Accommodation to present accepted papers at premier International/National Conferences.

Patent Filing Rewards

Financial reward of ₹25,000 upon successful grant of a National or International Patent, in addition to the institution covering all filing expenses.

Seed Money for Projects

Internal seed funding up to ₹2,000,000 provided to promising PhD scholars and faculty for initiating novel prototype development or pilot research models.

Best Researcher Award

Annual recognition and cash prize for the "Outstanding Scholar" based on cumulative academic performance, publications, and thesis milestones.

Book Authorship

Incentives granted for publishing academic books, monographs, or contributing chapters in text-books recognized by renowned international publishers.

For application forms and claim processes, consult your respective Head of the Department.
